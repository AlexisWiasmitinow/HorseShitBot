## 2. Key Architecture Decisions

1. **SLAM**: **SLAM Toolbox** (not Cartographer nor gmapping). Rationale: native ROS 2, offline mapping from rosbag, robustness against open-loop odometry. See `docs/slam_guide.md`.
2. **YOLO → Nav2**: no intermediate node. The `yolo_detector_node` projects detections (RGB + aligned depth) into `sensor_msgs/PointCloud2` directly consumable by the `local_costmap`.
3. **Odometry**: open-loop (integration of RPM commanded by the `wheel_driver_node`). AMCL and SLAM Toolbox are essential to correct drift.
4. **TensorRT**: the `scripts/convert_yolo_tensorrt.sh` script exports `.pt` → ONNX → `.engine` for fast inference on Jetson.

|---|--------------|-------------------|

## 5. Common Pitfalls

| Question | Quick Answer |
|----------|----------------|
| *Why does my `ros2 bag play` not publish anything in RViz?* | Check `use_sim_time:=true` on the SLAM/Nav2 side and `--clock` on the `ros2 bag play` side. |
| *Why doesn't Nav2 receive my odom?* | Verify that `odom` is the `frame_id` of the `Odometry` message and that the TF `odom → base_link` exists. |
| *Why is my costmap empty?* | The LiDAR must publish on `/scan` with the correct `frame_id` (`laser`). Check with `ros2 topic echo /scan | head`. |
| *Why doesn't the robot follow the path?* | The `controller_server` publishes on `/cmd_vel`. Check that `wheel_driver_node` is running and nothing else is publishing on `/cmd_vel`. |
| *Why is YOLO slow on the Jetson?* | Do not use the raw PyTorch `.pt`. Use the TensorRT `.engine` generated by `convert_yolo_tensorrt.sh`. |
| *Why don't YOLO obstacles appear in the local_costmap?* | Check that `/yolo/obstacles` publishes a `PointCloud2` and that its `header.frame_id` is known in the TF tree. |
